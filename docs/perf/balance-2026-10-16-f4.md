## Reading it

- **Gas:** about 9 cans a day for a base that defends at night and runs its factory by day, against 44 in the whole world. Nobody loots the whole map by night 3, so loot gas runs out around night 3-4. A fuel pump (Fuel line milestone) makes a night's worth in under 4 minutes: that's the factory's answer. Under the old rules the fences alone burned the world's gas in two days, a tax with no decision in it, which is why F4 part 2 arms fences at night (or with a zombie near) and runs the repair station only while it mends.
- **Nails:** a loud base's nights bring two or three hordes of up to 20, so 100-300 nails a night. 537 in the world, and walls, fences and turrets are built from the same nails. A body gives back 0.8 nails for the 5 it cost. Loot nails last about two nights; then the Press has to make them.
- **Wall panels:** 26 in the world, 4 for one wall rebuilt. A siege night uses 10-20, so about two nights; then the Press makes them (2 plates each).

So no numbers change beyond the power rules: with them, gas, nails and panels each give out from loot around night 2-4 for a base that fights, which is the roadmap's target. Re-run `tools/balance.sh` after changing loot tables, power draws or turret numbers.
