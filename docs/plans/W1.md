# W1 — Into the houses

*Plan by Claude Fable 5.1, 2026-10-12, from Emmitt's first play session ("there are no doors or ways into the buildings"). Build W1a first; W1b follows a playtest. One worktree and pull request each.*

## What the player gets

You walk up to a house, open the front door with E, and step into dark rooms with a fridge, cabinets and a bed inside. The loot is in there, not standing on the lawn. Closing the door behind you makes the street quieter and makes you harder to hear. Windows let you see out, and the dead can break them. You can board a window up with the barricade you already know, repair a wall, or knock an interior wall down. Later (W1b) some houses have a zombie waiting inside, the factory has a floor you can walk, and hordes come down the road.

## The one design decision

**Town buildings are made of the same 2 m grid pieces the player builds with.** Not special scenery. A house is foundation cells, wall edges, door edges, window edges and a roof, placed by code from a seed, exactly as if the player had built it. That is the whole trick: doors open and close, closed doors and walls already muffle sound (`BuildGrid.edge_blocks` and `NoiseField.walls_between`), zombies already bash through walls and doors, repair and barricades already work, the navmesh baker already reads built pieces, and saves already store pieces. The "town's buildings don't muffle yet" gap in the noise system closes by itself. Nothing in `src/sim` learns what a "house" is; only the generator does.

The alternative, hand-made house scenes in the Godot editor, would look better per hour of human work but can't be built in cloud sessions, can't be tested headlessly, and would need a second copy of every rule (doors, damage, muffling) for scenery. Rejected.

## W1a — houses you can enter (this milestone)

**New pieces in `data/buildables.json`** (all `player_placeable: false`; the build menu skips them; removing one is allowed and refunds scrap or planks rather than the piece):
- `town_floor` — a cell piece like `foundation` but flush with the ground, with a floorboard or concrete surface. Houses stand on it so interior walls have something to rest on.
- `town_wall` — an edge piece, hp 600, drawn with the house's brick or siding surface (not the corrugated scrap wall model). Blocks sound and sight like `wall`.
- `window` — an edge piece, hp 40. Intact: blocks sound like a wall, does not block sight. Broken (hp 0): becomes an opening (sound and sight pass, zombies and the player can go through) but stays as a piece so it can be boarded: placing `barricade` on a broken window edge replaces it. `edge_blocks(key, for_sight)` gains the window case; `walls_between` needs no change.
- `door` — the existing piece, used as is for front and back doors.
- A roof is not a sim piece. The world draws one flat slab per house at wall height (`BuildView` or a small `TownView`), with a ceiling underneath so rooms are dark and the flashlight matters. Sound is 2-D in this game, so a roof has no rule.

**Generator, `src/sim/town_plan.gd`** (`class_name TownPlan`, RefCounted, pure): `static func generate(seed: int, data: GameData) -> Dictionary` returning `{"pieces": [{id, cell, side}], "containers": [{id, table, cell}], "beds": [cell], "houses": [{cells, door_cell, door_side, name}]}`. Three floor plans on the grid, front door facing the road, a back door, three or four windows, one or two interior walls with open doorways (an edge left empty), containers inside on cells (kitchen cabinet and fridge in the kitchen, bathroom cabinet in the bathroom), a bed in the bedroom:
- A: 4×4 cells (8×8 m): living room, kitchen, bathroom, bedroom.
- B: 5×3 cells (10×6 m): kitchen-living room, bedroom, bathroom.
- C: L-shape, 5×4 minus a 2×2 corner.
Same twelve lots as today (six per side of the road at the same z positions), plan and mirror chosen by the seed, so the town keeps its shape. The factory stays a solid block in W1a.

**Placing it, in `main.gd._build_town`:** replace the twelve `_add_block` houses and their outside containers with the plan: `sim.build.restore(key, piece)` for each piece (no cost, no refund bookkeeping), `sim.loot.add` for each container at its indoor spot (the existing fridge and cabinet models, facing into the room), the bed as a real `bed` piece (sleeping in it sets your respawn point, as now), and the roof. **Saves:** town pieces live in `build.pieces`, so a save keeps damage, open doors, broken windows and removed walls. On load the generator must not run again when the save holds pieces (check how `SaveGame.apply` restores pieces today and follow it). Bump `SaveGame.VERSION` to 3 and keep loading version 2 with defaults, with the fixture test.

**Zombies and paths:** `NavBaker` already bakes from built pieces, so interiors get a navmesh and doorways become paths. Check that a zombie outside an open front door can reach a player inside, and that a closed door stops it until it breaks the door (the M8 rules). Keep the eight street zombies where they are.

**Hints:** add one tip after the crate tip: "Houses have food inside. E opens doors; close them behind you". Only on a new game, same rules as the others.

**Tests (headless, `tools/test.sh`):**
- `test_town_plan.gd`: same seed gives the same plan; every house has a front door on its road side; every container cell and bed cell is inside its house's cells; no two houses share a cell; the twelve lots are where the old houses were.
- `test_town_sound.gd`: from a continuous source inside a closed house, `walls_between` to the street is at least 1; opening the front door drops it; a broken window drops it; a barricade on the broken window restores it; the Director's `outdoor_level` for a smelter inside a closed house is lower than in the open.
- `test_window.gd`: window hp, break to opening, sight passes while sound is blocked when intact, barricade replaces a broken window and refunds nothing, you cannot place a window yourself.
- `test_town_world.gd` (physics): the player walks through an open front door into a house and is blocked by the interior wall; a zombie outside reaches the player inside through the open door; with the door closed it starts breaking it.
- `test_save.gd`: a version-2 fixture still loads; town damage and an open door survive save and load.
- Print the count of pieces placed and the time the first navmesh bake took, so the next session knows the cost.

**Screenshots to look at:** the street from the spawn (doors and windows visible on the houses), inside a house by day looking at the fridge, inside a house at night with the flashlight on (`--time=22 --flashlight` and a `--cam` inside a house; add a `--inside=N` flag if that is easier and list it in CLAUDE.md).

## W1b — life inside (after Playtest #1)

- A zombie standing in about one house in three, placed by the plan, idle until the door opens or it hears you.
- The factory as a grid interior: a 15×12-cell floor, big doorways (empty edges) at the road end, the three crates inside, a roof.
- Hordes follow the road: the Director's wave spawns walk the road's cells instead of a straight line (the road becomes a lane of navmesh they prefer).
- Furniture props from the Blender kit (table, couch, shelves) made on the PC and placed by the plan. Cloud sessions leave boxes.
- A fifth house plan and a shop with a different loot table.
