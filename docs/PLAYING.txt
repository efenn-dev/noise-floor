NOISE FLOOR - early test build
==============================

First-person survival and factory building. Scavenge the town, build machines and belts...
but machines make noise, and noise draws the dead.

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click NoiseFloor.exe.
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't signed.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway". (It's a small hobby game; signing costs money.)

You need a 64-bit Windows PC with a graphics card from the last ~8 years.

CONTROLS
--------
Press F1 in the game for the full list. The basics:
  W A S D      move           Mouse        look
  Shift        sprint         Ctrl         crouch (quieter)
  Left click   swing / shoot  Right click  aim or block
  E            use, search, open doors, sleep, get in cars
  Tab          inventory      B            build menu
  F            flashlight     Esc          pause and save

TIPS
----
- Eat and drink to stay alive; health slowly comes back when you're fed and watered,
  and fast when you sleep in a bed.
- Everything loud (machines, guns, cars, running) is heard by zombies. Night is worse.

Saves and play logs are kept in:  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Noise Floor\
The build you're playing is shown on the title screen. Please mention it when you send feedback.
