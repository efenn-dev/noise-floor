# Third-party assets

Everything here is CC0 (public domain) unless its licence column says otherwise. No credit is required for CC0; it's
listed so we know where each file came from. Paid assets (V37) are bought by Emmitt; their source files stay
local-only under C:/projects/assets-src and only what the game uses goes in the repo.
